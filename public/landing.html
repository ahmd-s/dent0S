FAQ

Questions answered.

Is DentOS suitable for a small single-chair clinic?
+
Absolutely. DentOS is built specifically for Indian dental practices — from solo practitioners to multi-chair clinics. The interface is designed to be fast and simple enough that even a non-technical receptionist can use it from day one.
How long does setup take?
+
Most clinics are live within the same day. We walk you through the setup personally — no IT required. You can import existing patient data, configure your settings, and start your first appointment within a few hours.
Do I need internet to use DentOS?
+
Yes, DentOS is a cloud-based web application. It works on any device with a browser — desktop, tablet, or mobile. An internet connection is required. We recommend a basic broadband connection for the best experience.
Is my clinic's data secure?
+
Yes. Patient data is encrypted and stored securely. DentOS follows healthcare data privacy best practices. Your data belongs to you — we never sell, share, or use it. You can export all your data at any time.
Can I try DentOS before paying?
+
Yes. We offer a free 20-minute live demo where we walk through DentOS using your actual clinic's workflow. No scripts, no pressure — just an honest look at what changes for you. Call or WhatsApp us to schedule.
What if I want to cancel?
+
No long-term contracts. Cancel any month. You keep your data. We'd prefer to keep you by being genuinely useful, not by trapping you in a commitment.
Does DentOS work for lab management too?
+
Yes. DentOS has a full lab workflow — clinics create lab cases, attach instructions, track status, and receive notifications when cases are ready. Labs also get their own view to manage and update cases. Full visibility for both sides.
